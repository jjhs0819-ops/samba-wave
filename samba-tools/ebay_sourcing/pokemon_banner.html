Condition
The photos are of the actual card you will receive — not a stock image. Please zoom in to check centering, edges, corners and surface before purchasing. Graded cards (PSA / BGS / CGC) are sold exactly as graded and cases are inspected for cracks prior to shipping.
What You Get
A single card unless the title says otherwise. Multi-variation listings ship the exact card you select from the drop-down.
Packing
Raw (ungraded) cards ship in a penny sleeve and a rigid top loader. Graded cards ship in a fitted case sleeve. Everything goes inside a bubble mailer marked "do not bend".
Shipping
Ships from South Korea with a tracking number, uploaded to eBay as soon as it is handed to the carrier. International transit typically takes 7–14 business days.
Customs, Duties & Import Taxes
Ships from South Korea. Where eBay's import-charge program applies (mainly the United States), duties and taxes are collected by eBay at checkout and nothing extra is due on delivery. For every other destination, any import duties, customs fees or taxes are set by your own country and are the buyer's responsibility — they are not included in the item price or the shipping fee, and unpaid charges are not grounds for a refund. Please check your country's import rules before ordering. We always declare the full value and never mark items as a gift.
Returns
30-day returns accepted if the card does not match the photos or description. Buyer pays return shipping. Please message us first — most issues are resolved without a formal return.
